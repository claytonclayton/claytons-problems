Context: This problem was written for UNSW CPMSoc's 2024 Term 1 UNSFW Contest, an april fool's contest full of joke questions. The joke for this question is that we have a long, convoluted and almost certainly NP-Hard problem statement, paired alongside constraints that produce a series of simultaneous equations. Turns out the contraints allow only one possible input, and so the problem reduces to a tractable math problem. The sample test case that the solutions do not pass on turns out not to be a valid input haha. Was fun and stressful to write this question.
